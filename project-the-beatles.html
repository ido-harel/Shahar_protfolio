the beatles

Year 2024
Role Print
Tools InDesign, Photoshop, Illustrator
project overview

This project presents a catalog dedicated to The Beatles, exploring their cultural impact through a structured editorial design that combines vintage references with contemporary clarity. The catalog examines the band’s evolution not only as musicians but as cultural icons who shaped fashion, youth culture, politics, and visual aesthetics throughout the 1960s and beyond..
